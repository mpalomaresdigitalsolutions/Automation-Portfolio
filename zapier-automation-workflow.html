System 02 · CRM & Database Orchestration

Google Forms & Airtable Two-Way Lead Sync

🟢 Real-Time Synchronization

A resilient bi-directional data orchestration engine. Captures incoming leads from Google Forms, validates and formats customer attributes, writes records to Airtable for sales pipeline management, and synchronizes to Google Sheets as a historical data backup.

🔄 Bi-Directional Integrity

The Problem: Disconnected systems result in duplicate records, outdated lead statuses, and messy manual exports between teams.
The Solution: Real-time syncing guarantees that sales reps working in Airtable and analysts reviewing Google Sheets always view identical records.

🛡️ Zero Data Loss Fallback

The Problem: When a single CRM service undergoes maintenance or rate limiting, incoming customer submissions get dropped.
The Solution: Dual-write architecture logs form submissions simultaneously to Airtable and secondary Google Sheets storage.
