Google Ads vs Local Services Ads (LSA) for Contractors

An objective comparison of pay-per-click vs. pay-per-lead: cost differences, dispute policies, keyword intent control, and how top contractors run both together.

By Marlon Palomares · Last updated: September 2026 · 7 min read

Quick Answer & Executive Summary In 2026, Google Local Services Ads (LSA) operates on a pay-per-lead model with the Google Guaranteed badge, ideal for baseline inbound phone calls with minimal setup. In contrast, Google Search Ads (PPC) operates on a pay-per-click model, providing complete control over landing pages, negative keywords, ad copy, and high-ticket service targeting (such as full roof replacements or whole-home repiping). The highest-performing home service companies run both concurrently to achieve SERP Dominance, backed by GoHighLevel automated call tracking to dispute invalid LSA leads and immediately qualify PPC inquiries.

2. When Should You Use Local Services Ads?

Google Local Services Ads is best for capturing immediate emergency phone calls at a predictable cost per call. If you are a plumbing, HVAC, or electrical contractor with active insurance, background checks, and strong Google reviews (at least 20+ reviews and a 4.6+ rating), LSA is an essential foundational channel.

3. Why You Still Need Standard Google Ads

Contractors who rely 100% on LSA often encounter a major plateau:

  • LSA Call Throttling: Google's LSA algorithm rotates leads among competitors. Once you hit your weekly allocation, lead flow can abruptly stall.
  • Inability to Target High-Ticket Projects: In LSA, you cannot tell Google: "Only bid on $15,000 whole-house replumbing or commercial HVAC replacements." Google sends calls for simple drain clogs and high-margin replacements indiscriminately.
  • Conversion Rate Control: With Google Search Ads, you direct traffic to dedicated GoHighLevel landing pages featuring video reviews, before-and-after photos, and financing calculators, producing much higher qualification rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Local Services Ads (LSA) better than Google Search Ads?

Neither is universally better; they serve different purposes. LSA charges strictly per call and carries the Google Guaranteed badge, while Google Search Ads gives contractors full control over specific keywords, landing page offers, and high-ticket service targeting.

Can contractors run Google Ads and Local Services Ads at the same time?

Yes, running both simultaneously is the optimal strategy. This approach allows a contractor to capture the Google Guaranteed badge AND the top search ad position, controlling over 60% of above-the-fold search real estate.

Why do contractors still need Google Ads if LSA exists?

LSA algorithms frequently throttle call volume once budgets peak, and LSA does not allow targeting specific high-margin services. Google Ads allows precise control to target high-ticket jobs.
