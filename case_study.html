Junk Removal
Lead Machine.

A US junk removal company needed consistent inbound calls and form fills — not impressions. I built and managed Google Search campaigns around high-intent keywords that delivered qualified leads at a profitable cost per acquisition, sustained on a $10K/month budget.

01 · The Challenge

High volume, zero structure

Junk removal is a fast-moving, local service business. Customers need same-week pickups, compare quickly, and pick the first company that answers.

Competitive local market

Major national brands and local operators were bidding on the same service terms. Without precision targeting and tight ad groups, budget disappeared into low-intent searches.

Speed wins the job

Whoever calls back first gets the haul. The account needed to generate real phone calls and form fills — not clicks from people comparing prices for weeks.

Tracking had to be bulletproof

No conversion tracking, no optimization. Call tracking and form submission tracking were set up before a single dollar was optimized.

02 · The Strategy

Built to capture high-intent demand

A lead machine is only as good as its targeting. Every campaign was engineered around who is ready to book — right now.

High-intent keyword architecture

Focused on transactional and service-specific searches — "junk removal near me", "same day junk pickup", "get rid of junk" — grouped into tightly themed ad groups that match the customer's exact intent.

Local & service-based targeting

Geo-targeted to the service area, with negative keywords filtering out DIY searches and people outside the operating zone. Every impression was spent on someone who could actually book.

Conversion-first measurement

Call tracking, form fills, and GHL automation captured every lead source. Once we knew what converted, budget followed the winners and losers got paused.

Scale what the data says

Weekly optimization cycles: scale profitable keywords, add negatives, refine bids — keeping cost per acquisition profitable as volume grew toward the full $10K/mo budget.

03 · The Result

Consistent, profitable leads

The account settled into a repeatable flow — high-intent searches turning into inbound calls and form fills at a CPA the client could scale on.

A machine that repeats

With conversion data driving every decision, the campaign delivered steady inbound lead flow month over month at a sustainable cost per acquisition — built to scale with additional budget.
