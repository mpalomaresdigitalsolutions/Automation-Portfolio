01 · The Challenge

High volume, zero structure

Junk removal is a fast-moving, local service business. Customers need same-week pickups, compare quickly, and pick the first company that answers.

🌍
Competitive Local Market
Major national brands and local operators bid on the same service terms. Without precision targeting and tight ad groups, budget disappeared into low-intent searches.
⚡
Speed Wins the Job
Whoever calls back first gets the haul. The account needed to generate real phone calls and form fills — not clicks from people comparing prices for weeks.
📊
Flawed Conversion Tracking
The most critical flaw: no reliable call and form tracking, so the campaign had no idea what actually booked jobs — every optimization decision was made on misleading data.
💸
Substantial Budget Waste
The combined effect: wasted ad spend with no mechanism to identify what was working — budget leaking into unqualified traffic every day.

03 · The Strategy

Optimization framework:
7-step methodology

Executed comprehensive campaign restructuring through targeted optimization initiatives — each addressing a specific inefficiency in the original account.

01 / 07
📉
Geographic Targeting Refinement
Eliminated geographic inefficiencies by implementing precise location targeting to the service area, removing out-of-zone traffic waste, and optimizing for qualified regional audiences aligned with actual pickup availability.
02 / 07
💡
Precision Keyword Architecture
Replaced broad-match inefficiencies with targeted long-tail keywords like "junk removal near me" and "same day junk pickup" — grouped into tightly themed ad groups that match high-intent booking behavior.
03 / 07
🚫
Traffic Quality Enhancement
Implemented strategic negative keyword expansion and network exclusions to filter out DIY searches and out-of-zone browsers, improving overall campaign efficiency and spend quality.
04 / 07
📍
Audience Segmentation Optimization
Established precise demographic and behavioral targeting parameters to ensure ad delivery only to homeowners and businesses actively seeking removal services within the operational geography.
05 / 07
🔍
Conversion Attribution Framework
Developed comprehensive tracking infrastructure aligned to phone calls and form submissions, enabling accurate performance measurement and strategic optimization instead of optimizing on vanity clicks.
06 / 07
⚙️
Continuous Optimization Protocol
Established systematic performance monitoring with weekly optimization cycles, keyword refinement, and bid strategy adjustments for sustained, compounding performance improvement.
07 / 07
🔗
Automation & CRM Integration
Implemented GoHighLevel call tracking and follow-up automation so every inbound lead is captured, routed, and contacted immediately — because the first company to answer wins the job.

The click volume paradox

More is not always better. By eliminating hundreds of low-intent clicks from DIY searchers and out-of-zone browsers, we redirected that budget to high-intent customers ready to book. Quality always beats quantity in paid search. Would you rather have 1,000 random visitors who never call, or 100 qualified leads ready to schedule a pickup?

A machine that repeats

With call and form tracking driving every decision, the campaign now delivers steady, qualified inbound lead flow at a sustainable cost per acquisition — built to scale with additional budget and expand across the full service territory.
