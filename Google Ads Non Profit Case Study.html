Section 01 · Diagnostics & Initial Audit

The Challenge: Flying Blind & Burning Budget

⚠️ Pre-Optimization Audit

Humanity Impact Institute faced critical inefficiencies in their Google Ads account architecture. Four systemic structural flaws were draining budget and blinding performance measurement.

🌍 1 · Geographic Targeting Mismatch

Ads were broadcasting internationally to users across Europe and North America for Philippine-based on-site internship programs. Over 40% of clicks had zero operational eligibility to apply.

🔍 2 · Broad Keyword Bleed

Broad-match keywords attracted generic searches like general volunteer blogs and charity news, driving irrelevant clicks with zero application intent.

📊 3 · Fatal Attribution Tracking Flaw

The most critical flaw: The account conversion goal was set to track financial donations instead of internship application completions. Google's Smart Bidding was optimizing for donors, while the organization desperately needed qualified student interns.

💸 4 · $847+ Direct Budget Waste

Without negative keyword lists or localized radius targeting, over $847 was burned on non-converting international bot traffic before our optimization took place.

In paid search, volume without qualification is a budget trap. Section 04 · Execution Methodology

The 7-Step Google Ads Optimization Framework

🟢 Step-by-Step Playbook

The exact technical roadmap executed to eliminate waste, restore attribution accuracy, and automate applicant follow-up with CRM integration.

Step 01 / 07
Geographic Targeting Refinement
Eliminated international traffic bleed by restricting ad delivery to qualified regions where candidates can physically participate.
Step 02 / 07
Precision Keyword Architecture
Replaced broad match with high-intent phrase and exact match keywords focused specifically on student internship searches.
Step 03 / 07
Negative Keyword Expansion
Added 120+ negative keywords to filter out job seekers looking for salaried corporate employment, international scholarships, or unrelated degrees.
Step 04 / 07
Demographic & Age Calibration
Calibrated bid adjustments for university student age cohorts (18–24 and 25–34) to prioritize core eligible applicants.
Step 05 / 07
Conversion Attribution Realignment
Re-architected Google Ads conversion actions using Google Tag Manager to trigger solely on verified internship application submissions.
Step 06 / 07
Weekly Bid & Query Optimization
Instituted weekly search term audits, bid pacing, and Quality Score monitoring to ensure budget compounds in efficiency over time.
Step 07 / 07
Zapier & GoHighLevel Automation
Integrated form submissions with Zapier to push applicants instantly into GoHighLevel CRM, triggering automated SMS confirmation and interview links.
