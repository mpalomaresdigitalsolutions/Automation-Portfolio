1. The Unit Economics of Roofing Pay-Per-Click

Roofing keywords are among the highest-priced clicks in local search ($35 to $75+ per click). Why? Because a single signed contract generates $12,000 to $25,000 in gross revenue. One closed deal pays for an entire month of marketing.

However, running roofing Google Ads without disciplined keyword match types and negative lists is disastrous. Searchers looking for "roof repair DIY", "roofing shingles wholesale price", or "roofing jobs near me" will rapidly drain your budget with zero intent of hiring a contractor.

2. Retail Replacement vs. Storm Damage Architecture

A high-performing roofing campaign separates two distinct buying mindsets:

  • Retail Roof Replacement: Homeowners noticing aging roofs, missing shingles, or planning home renovations. Highly influenced by warranty terms (e.g., GAF Master Elite, Owens Corning), financing options ($0 down, $199/month), and verified customer reviews.
  • Storm, Hail & Wind Damage: Triggered immediately following weather events. Homeowners need urgent tarping, drone damage inspections, and assistance navigating insurance claim adjustments.
