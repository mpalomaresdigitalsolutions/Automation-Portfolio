1. The Real Cost of HVAC Pay-Per-Click Advertising

HVAC is among the most competitive local service categories on Google. Unlike general remodeling where customers shop around for weeks, an air conditioner failure during a 95°F heatwave requires immediate resolution. Homeowners click the top 1–2 search results and call immediately.

Because click costs can reach $50 to $110 per click for high-urgency keywords (e.g., "emergency ac repair near me", "24 hour heating repair"), unmonitored broad match keywords will bleed thousands in wasted spend on searchers seeking DIY thermostat diagrams or warranty phone numbers.

2. Separating High-Ticket System Replacements from Low-Margin Tune-Ups

A winning HVAC Google Ads architecture splits campaigns into dedicated intent silos:

  • Emergency Service & Repair: Maximizes phone call conversions with localized 15-mile radiuses around technician service hubs.
  • Full System Replacement / Heat Pumps: Targets homeowners planning major replacements ($8,000–$16,000 ticket sizes) using finance calculators and rebates.
  • Seasonal Maintenance Specials: Run during shoulder seasons (spring/fall) with lower target CPA caps to keep technicians booked between weather spikes.
