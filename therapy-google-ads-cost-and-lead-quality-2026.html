How much does Google Ads management cost for a therapy practice?

Therapy advertising balances demand, privacy, geography, clinician capacity, and intake quality. A 2026 budget should start with the number of new clients the practice can serve and the value of a completed intake—not an arbitrary lead target.

What makes therapy campaign measurement different?

A form fill is not the same as an attended intake. Track inquiry source, service fit, location, and whether the practice could contact the person. Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive health details in ad forms or sending them to third parties.

Landing-page trust and clarity

Explain who the service is for, what the first step looks like, availability, credentials, privacy expectations, and how to request an appointment. Avoid unsupported clinical promises.

How to improve lead quality

Use specific service language, negative keywords, service-area controls, clear expectations, and intake-stage measurement. Review fit and attended appointments, not only cost per form.

Questions prospective clients ask

What is a realistic therapy Google Ads budget?

It depends on local competition, clinician capacity, session economics, and qualified-intake targets.

Can therapy practices use lead forms?

They can, but forms should avoid unnecessary sensitive information and follow applicable privacy and platform policies.

How do therapy practices reduce low-quality leads?

Use specific service language, location boundaries, clear expectations, negative keywords, and intake-stage measurement.

Need a campaign plan?
Bring your service area, average job value, close rate, and current conversion tracking. The first decision is usually which lead and revenue signals the campaign should optimize for.
